---
name: unload-card
description: Use when drafting a kiln unload card or handing off a cooling tray.
---

A card is ready when the next shift can find the tray and tell whether it may move it.

1. Read the cooling tray label for its shelf and load number.
2. Put the shelf, load number, and cooling time on the card. If the cooling time is unverified, write `hold for kiln lead` instead of guessing.
3. Read those three fields back before handing off the card.
